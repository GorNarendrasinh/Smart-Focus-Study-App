                                     ⚡ Smart Study Focus-App

🚀 Smart Study Focus-App is a modern, intelligent productivity platform built to help students, professionals, and lifelong learners stay focused, organized, and motivated. By combining task management, Pomodoro focus technique, AI-powered notes analysis, and smart progress tracking, this app ensures you maximize productivity while learning efficiently — all in a clean, distraction-free interface.

                                    🔹Titles for Features Section

🏠 Home Page – View daily tasks, notes, and study progress at a glance.

👤 User Dashboard – Personalized overview of tasks, study stats, Pomodoro sessions, and AI insights.

🔐 User Registration & Authentication – Secure Django-based signup, login, and logout functionality.

✅ To-Do List – Add, edit, prioritize, and track your tasks efficiently.

⏳ Pomodoro Timer – Boost focus with 25-minute work sessions and short breaks.

📅 Schedule Page – Plan weekly/monthly tasks and get reminders for deadlines.

🤖 AI Notes & Analytics – Summarize notes, highlight key points, and get actionable insights.

📚 AI Books Page – Access recommended books with summaries and key takeaways.

🎯 Distraction-Free Mode – Minimize interruptions and focus deeply on tasks.

📊 Progress Tracking – Visual graphs to monitor completed tasks, sessions, and achievements.

                               🚀 Core Features.

🏠 Home Page

Daily Tasks Overview – Quickly view and manage all your tasks for the day.
Notes Dashboard – Organize all your study notes in one place.
Daily Study Graphs – Visualize your daily, weekly, and monthly progress.
Motivational Insights – Personalized productivity tips and study reminders.

👤 User Dashboard & Authentication

Secure Registration & Login – Django-based signup, login, and logout functionality.
Personalized Dashboard – After login, users get a custom dashboard showing tasks, Pomodoro sessions, AI notes insights, and progress.
Manage Account – Users can update profile and track their own study statistics.

✅ To-Do Page

Smart Task Management – Add, edit, prioritize, and categorize tasks effortlessly.
Track Progress – Mark tasks complete and monitor productivity over time.

⏳ Pomodoro Timer

Focus Sessions – Boost concentration with 25-minute work sprints and short breaks.
Customizable Intervals – Adjust work and break durations to suit your routine.
Session History – Track past Pomodoro sessions to analyze focus patterns.

📅 Schedule Page

Plan Ahead – Create weekly/monthly study/work schedules.
Reminders & Alerts – Never miss a task, deadline, or study goal.
Visual Calendar – Organize your time with a clear calendar view.

🤖 AI Notes & Analytics

Smart Summaries – Automatically condense long notes into key points.
Highlight Key Info – Quickly identify the most important study material.
AI Insights – Suggest improvements for study habits and learning efficiency.

📚 AI Books Page

Recommended Reads – Access AI-curated books and study resources.
Quick Summaries – Summarize chapters and books for fast learning.
Actionable Insights – Extract key highlights to optimize your study sessions.

🎯 Distraction-Free Mode

Stay Focused – Minimize interruptions and maintain deep concentration.
Clean Interface – Simple, minimal design to keep your mind on tasks.

📊 Progress Tracking

Visual Analytics – Track daily, weekly, and monthly performance with charts.
Monitor Productivity – Review completed tasks, Pomodoro sessions, and notes analyzed.
Goal Motivation – Set measurable goals and stay consistently motivated.

🛠️ Technologies Used

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
Backend: Python, Django
Database: Django Admin (for structured data storage)
AI Integration: Notes summarization, analytics, and insights
